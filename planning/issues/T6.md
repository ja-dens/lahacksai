## Outcome

Build an independent evaluation harness, held-out synthetic dataset and reporting rubric for context/personalization/uncertainty. The deliverable is usable immediately with saved outputs or an injected fake interpreter; measured live results are a later handoff.

## Ownership and boundary

Role B or a separate evaluation teammate; preserve actual assignees. Own evaluation/, scripts/evaluate_stickers.py, tests/test_evaluation.py and docs/EVALUATION.md. Do not edit prompt/model/core modules while preparing held-out examples.

Inputs: v2 request fixtures, labeled example metadata and saved InterpretationResult JSON, or an injected callable interpreter. Outputs: deterministic comparison/report files with case counts, label provenance, errors, missing/unmeasured fields and explicitly marked fixture/live modes. No Photon account, deployed backend or model credits are needed for fixture mode.
