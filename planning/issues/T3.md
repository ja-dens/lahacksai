## Outcome

Build a replaceable multimodal adapter and interpretation policy that accept a frozen v2 request and return a validated result. Develop and complete the module without Photon or a running database.

## Ownership and boundary

Role B; preserve the existing assignee. Own src/interpreter.jac, src/adapters/vision.py, prompts/sticker.txt and tests/test_interpreter.py. Input: interpretation_request.json plus actual synthetic PNG bytes. Output: InterpretationResult. Inject a fake SDK client/HTTP transport and use the candidate fixtures; never make the model choose a delivery destination.

A local workspace already has policy and mock-SDK checks, recorded below. Their application source is not yet published in this planning update; reconcile reviewed local work rather than assuming a fresh clone contains it.

- [ ] Use at most two strongest ranked candidates, relation (single/compatible/opposing/none), rankability, a short context cue and uncertainty caveat. No extra model call is required for personalization.
